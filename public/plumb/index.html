Before you research candidates, make sure you can actually vote. These are universal starting points — they route you to your own state's official sites.

Are you registered?

Check your registration status
Use vote.org for a universal check, or usa.gov/voter-registration to register if not. Most states require registration ~30 days before election day. North Dakota is the exception (no registration). California, Michigan, Minnesota, and others allow same-day registration.

Find your sample ballot

Your state's Secretary of State
Search "[your state] secretary of state sample ballot" or your county election office. Most states publish your specific sample ballot 2-4 weeks before election day, keyed to your address. Print it OR keep the PDF open in a tab — you'll type the races + candidates into the next step.

Universal research starting points

Sources Plumb will deep-link to per candidate
  • Ballotpedia — best for state-legislative + federal; thinner on local
  • Vote Smart — federal positions, voting records, ratings from advocacy groups
  • Candidate's own site — what they want you to know
  • Local news search — what's been reported, what controversies exist
  • Official filings — financial disclosures, voting records

Honest note on the "ill-informed shouldn't vote" instinct

Don't skip voting because you can't research everything. If your principles are clear, a 30%-research vote is still a 100%-principled vote. Plumb's job: stabilize your principles so any amount of research time yields a values-aligned ballot.

Write your principles before you meet the candidates. A principle is a single claim you agree or disagree with — plus how much it weighs in your decision. Stable across elections. Edit when your thinking changes; don't edit in the middle of a race.

Examples: "Government should provide a safety net for the elderly." "Religious institutions should be free of state interference." "Abortion should be legal in the first trimester." "The death penalty is wrong." Be specific. Vague principles produce vague filtering.
