Slow letters

Write something today. Send it forward in time. The reader can't unseal it before the date you set.

Open as recipient

Send this URL however — text, email, paper print. The reader can hold it, but cannot unseal it before your chosen date.

Most messages today travel at the speed of light. Longitude makes them travel at the speed of waiting.

You write a letter. You set a delivery date. The app mints a URL that contains the letter, the date, and an integrity seal. You send the URL — by text, by email, by printing it onto a card.

The recipient holds the URL. When they open it before the delivery date, all they see is a sealed envelope and a countdown. When they open it after, the letter is there.

No server holds your message. The whole letter lives inside the URL. Lose the URL, lose the letter.
